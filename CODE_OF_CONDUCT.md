# Code of Conduct

## Commitment

Bulk Mail Project welcomes participation from people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. We aim to keep discussions respectful, constructive, and focused on improving the project.

## Expected behavior

- Treat contributors and reviewers with respect.
- Give and receive feedback in good faith.
- Focus critique on code, design, and technical claims.
- Respect privacy and do not publish private information.
- Follow the project's security, contribution, and responsible-email guidance.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing, and disruptive behavior that prevents others from participating are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Maintainer response

The maintainer may remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation when needed to keep collaboration safe and useful. Responses should be proportionate to the behavior.

For conduct concerns, contact the maintainer privately through GitHub [@brajesh31](https://github.com/brajesh31). Do not post private recipient or account information in public issues.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project-related spaces, including when someone represents the project publicly. It supports respectful collaboration and does not prohibit good-faith technical disagreement.
